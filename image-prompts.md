# Gameplay Mockup Prompts

These are image-generation prompts for mock screenshots of the three game ideas in [top-3-concepts.md](top-3-concepts.md), showing what each would look like while you play. Each image is a first-person view with the weapon in hand and the on-screen display (HUD) visible, as if captured during a game. Use them for pitching, mood boards, or a visual target before running the build.

## The three concepts

1. **[Do Not Feed the Kea](#1-do-not-feed-the-kea)**
2. **[Population: 1](#2-population-1)**
3. **[Special Votes](#3-special-votes)**

## How to use these prompts

- **Three screenshots per concept, all 16:9:**
  - **A. Mid-wave:** the main action, mid-fight.
  - **B. The twist:** the mechanic that makes this concept different.
  - **C. Objective moment:** the closing moment of the round, a win or a near-loss.
- **Build each prompt from four blocks.** Paste the [shared style block](#shared-style-block), then the concept's **look block**, then its **HUD block**, then the screenshot prompt. Use the [shared negative prompt](#shared-negative-prompt) where the tool supports one.
- **Which tools.** The prompts are written in plain English, so they work in most image generators. For Midjourney, add `--ar 16:9`. For tools without negative prompts, add "no gore, no real logos" at the end.
- **On-screen text.** Image generators often garble lettering. The HUD blocks ask for short labels only. Put the real wording in afterwards with an image editor, using each concept's **HUD and sign text** list.
- **Guardrails.** These follow the [rules in top-3-concepts.md](top-3-concepts.md#rules-for-all-three-concepts): no real people, brands, parties or government logos, and no harm to native wildlife. Animals are cheeky and annoying, never hurt.

## Shared style block

```
In-game screenshot of a real-time 3D first-person game, captured during play, 1920x1080. High-end indie game look:
physically based materials with subtle weathering, soft shadows, filmic colour grade, gentle bloom, light
atmospheric haze, slight motion in particles. First-person camera at eye height, the player's weapon and gloved
hands in the lower right of the frame. A clean, minimal game HUD overlaid on the image. Deadpan comedic tone:
everything is rendered seriously, only the situation is absurd.
```

## Shared negative prompt

```
poster, title card, key art, third-person view, character portrait, concept sketch, illustration, painting,
garbled HUD text, watermark, real brand logos, real political party logos, government crests, real people,
gore, blood, injured animals, dead animals, cartoon exaggeration, anime style, oversaturated colours,
fisheye distortion, extra fingers, deformed hands
```

## 1. Do Not Feed the Kea

**Look block:**
```
Snowy car park at a New Zealand alpine mountain pass in late spring: overcast sky, light snow falling, old snow on
grey scree, dark green native beech forest on the slopes, low cloud on the ridges, cold blue-grey light with warm
orange accents from road cones and barriers. Three white rental campervans with plain unbranded stripes parked
in the bays.
```

**HUD block:**
```
HUD: top left, a small objective panel listing three campervans, each with a short damage bar. Top centre, a wave
counter. Bottom left, a ranger badge icon and a stamina bar. Bottom right next to the weapon, a fuel gauge for the
leaf blower. A small crosshair in the centre. Flat, semi-transparent dark panels with white and safety-orange text.
```

**HUD and sign text to add later:**
- Objective panel: "VAN 1: WIPERS 2/2", "VAN 2: WIPERS 1/2", "VAN 3: SEALS 64%"
- Wave counter: "WAVE 2 OF 3"
- Alert: "AFTERSHOCK: DROP, COVER, HOLD"
- Kill-feed style lines: "Kea relocated (temporarily)", "Possum trapped. Predator Free 2050: 0.0001%"
- Road sign: "DO NOT FEED THE KEA", "ROAD CLOSED: AFTERSHOCKS"

### 1A. Mid-wave
```
The player holds a petrol leaf blower in the lower right, nozzle forward, gloved hands on the handle. A blast of air
and swirling snow knocks a startled kea off the roof of the nearest campervan; the olive-green parrot tumbles
backwards unharmed, wings spread, flashing orange-red underwings, looking offended. Two more kea are perched on
the van's wipers. Further back, a brushtail possum runs across the snow toward a wooden trap box. Kill-feed lines in
the top right. Snow particles streak past the camera.
```

### 1B. The twist: aftershock
```
The camera is low to the ground and tilted slightly, as if the player has just crouched behind a concrete barrier.
The leaf blower is lowered. A large safety-orange alert banner fills the top of the HUD with a shaking-ground icon.
Light motion blur and camera shake; snow and grit lift off the ground; a road cone has toppled. Kea have taken off
in a startled flock above the campervans. A crack runs across the asphalt in front of the barrier.
```

### 1C. Objective moment: the road reopens
```
Late in the round. The player stands at the car park exit looking down the mountain road. A road-closed barrier
is swinging open, its orange lamp flashing. The leaf blower's fuel gauge is nearly empty. In the HUD, all three van
damage bars are low but not empty. On top of the barrier, a single kea is holding a traffic cone in its beak,
staring at the camera. Snow is easing off and a sliver of brighter sky shows through the clouds.
```

## 2. Population: 1

**Look block:**
```
Main street of a tiny rural New Zealand town in spring: old wooden shopfronts with verandahs and corrugated-iron
roofs, a small corner shop ("dairy"), a pub, an old single petrol pump on a forecourt, a small war memorial, cabbage
trees, yellow gorse flowering on the hills behind, bright afternoon sun with big white clouds, warm slightly faded
colours. The town is completely deserted.
```

**HUD block:**
```
HUD: top left, an objective panel with three icons (shop, pub, pump), each with a short status bar. Top right, a
small town welcome-sign icon with a population number. Top centre, a wave counter. Bottom left, a health bar
shaped like a pie. Bottom right next to the weapon, a tennis-ball ammo counter. A small crosshair in the centre.
Flat, slightly retro panels in cream and dark green.
```

**HUD and sign text to add later:**
- Objective panel: "DAIRY: OPEN", "PUB: OPEN", "PUMP: $3.49/L"
- Population: "POP. 1"
- Wave counter: "WAVE 3 OF 3"
- Pop-ups: "Petrol up 12c", "Neighbour moved to Perth", "Magpie hit. Magpie unbothered."
- Shop sign: "Back in 5 mins"; banner: "Haere rā, good luck in Perth!"
- Ending choice: "Job offer: Perth. [YEAH] [NAH]"

### 2A. Mid-wave
```
The player holds a tennis-ball launcher in the lower right, a tennis ball mid-flight toward a black-and-white
magpie that is swooping straight at the camera, wings wide and beak open. The top edge of the frame shows the rim
of a plastic ice-cream container worn as a helmet. Ahead, the dairy with a hand-written cardboard sign in the window,
and rabbits nibbling the grass verge beside the petrol pump. A faded farewell banner hangs across the street.
```

### 2B. The twist: the price goes up
```
The player looks at the old petrol pump on the forecourt; its large mechanical price display is mid-flip to a
higher number. A pop-up in the HUD announces the price rise. On the pump's roof, three magpies stand in a row,
watching. The tennis-ball launcher is lowered, nearly out of balls. A rabbit sits in the middle of the forecourt,
unbothered. Bright, flat afternoon light; long shadows.
```

### 2C. Objective moment: the job offer
```
The player stands in the middle of the empty main street at golden hour, looking at a phone held in their left
hand. The phone screen shows a simple dialog with two large buttons. In the HUD, all three objective bars are
still green and the population reads 1. Behind the phone, the pub's lights have just come on. A single magpie is
perched on the welcome sign at the end of the street.
```

## 3. Special Votes

**Look block:**
```
Interior of an old New Zealand community hall at night: wooden floorboards, a small stage with heavy faded
curtains, varnished wooden honours boards on the walls, stacked plastic chairs, long trestle tables piled with
ballot papers and plain cardboard ballot boxes, flickering fluorescent tube lights, rain streaking the tall windows.
Cool green-white light with warm wood tones.
```

**HUD block:**
```
HUD: top left, an objective panel with three counting tables, each with a progress bar. Top centre, a large
countdown clock. Top right, a tally with two equal numbers side by side. Bottom left, a lanyard ID-card icon with a
tiredness meter. Bottom right next to the weapon, an ink-pad level. A small crosshair in the centre. Plain
official-looking panels in grey and pale blue.
```

**HUD and sign text to add later:**
- Objective panel: "TABLE 1: 100%", "TABLE 2: 73%", "TABLE 3: 41%"
- Clock: "03:12 UNTIL DEADLINE"
- Tally: "50.0% | 50.0%"
- Pop-ups: "Ballot returned to correct box", "Hoarding removed. Another one appeared."
- Election signs (fictional parties): "Getting On With It", "Back On Track Forward"
- Hall banner: "SPECIAL VOTES: COUNT IN PROGRESS"

### 3A. Mid-wave
```
The player holds a large rubber date stamp in the lower right, mid-swing. A loose ballot paper flutters toward the
camera through the air. Dozens more swirl above the trestle tables, blown by a wall-mounted heat pump with its
louvres jammed open. Two election signs on wooden stakes, in bright made-up party colours with abstract logos,
shuffle across the floorboards toward the camera like penguins. Pop-up messages in the top right.
```

### 3B. The twist: the heat pump
```
The player faces the wall heat pump up close; it is roaring, with visible air distortion and a stream of ballot
papers pouring out of a nearby cardboard box and into the air. A HUD warning flashes beside the heat pump with a
fan icon. The date stamp is raised. Fluorescent tubes flicker overhead; the rain outside the windows is heavy.
Ballots are stuck to the honours boards.
```

### 3C. Objective moment: too close to call
```
The countdown clock in the HUD reads zero. The player looks across the hall at the stage, where two election signs
in different colours stand side by side under a single spotlight. On the trestle table in front of the camera,
two piles of ballot papers of exactly equal height. The tally in the HUD shows two identical numbers. A paper cup
of tea has gone cold on the table. The date stamp rests on the table, out of ink.
```
